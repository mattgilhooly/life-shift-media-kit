Why this show exists

I lost my mom when I was eight years old. That loss shaped most of what came after, and it is the reason I started The Life Shift.

Every episode is one person and one moment, told in their own words. The listener hears that someone else has stood where they are standing, and that they are not alone.

The show is independent and I own it. No network decides who gets to tell their story.

What a partnership makes possible

The Life Shift has no network and no sales team. Partner support goes straight back into the show.

  • Better productionCleaner sound, stronger editing, and episodes that look and sound the way these stories deserve.
  • An independent show that keeps runningTwo new conversations a week, with time to prepare for each one.
  • Reaching more listenersMarketing that puts these stories in front of people who need to hear them.
  • Award submissionsEntering the show where it can be recognized, and carrying that recognition to partners.
